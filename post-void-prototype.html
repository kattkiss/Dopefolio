02 / DESIGN VISION

Creating pressure without restricting player freedom

I wanted to explore a different approach to tension in fast-paced shooters. Rather than relying primarily on claustrophobic level layouts, my goal was to create urgency through systems that encouraged continuous movement and quick decisions instead.

The Main Design Challenge

How can a fast-paced FPS encourage players to balance speed, safety, and reward while still allowing freedom of movement?

My approach

01 / URGENCY

Make time a limited resource

A constantly decreasing timer pushes players to keep moving.

02 / RISK AND REWARD

Make encounters a decision

Fighting enemies can provide additional time through pickups, but avoiding danger may offer a safer route.

03 / PLAYER FREEDOM

Support different routes

Open spaces, verticality, and movement options give players opportunities to choose how they approach the level.

03 / GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS

Connecting urgency, combat, and survival

I designed the timer, pickups, and combat systems to support a shared goal: encourage players to make quick decisions under pressure.

The starting timer is deliberately shorter than the time required to finish a level, making time recovery necessary.

04 / MOVEMENT DESIGN

Giving players more control over traversal

04 / MOVEMENT

Sliding adds momentum and terrain-based variation

The crouch input changes behaviour depending on whether the player is sprinting. When sliding, terrain affects speed, creating opportunities for level design to shape movement.

This allowed movement mechanics to influence level design, rather than existing as isolated features.

05 / LEVEL DESIGN

Designing spaces around movement

Connecting layout and gameplay

The level design was intended to complement the game's time pressure rather than relying entirely on restrictive spaces. By incorporating movement opportunities, environmental obstacles, and alternating spatial layouts, I wanted to encourage different approaches to traversal.

06 / TECHNICAL WORK

Preventing repeated knockback

IMPLEMENTATION EXAMPLE · ENEMY COLLISIONS

Collision response and cooldown

When an enemy collides with the player, it launches them upwards and backwards, temporarily interrupting player control.

I implemented a cooldown between knockback events to prevent the same interaction from continuously affecting the player.

01 / Collision Enemy makes contact with the player.
02 / Response Apply knockback and interrupt control.
03 / Cooldown Prevent another immediate knockback.
07 / REFLECTIONS

What I learned

This project was primarily an opportunity to explore a new engine and develop my understanding of visual scripting while experimenting with fast-paced gameplay design.

Learning Blueprints

I developed a foundational understanding of Unreal Engine's Blueprint system through implementing gameplay mechanics.

Scope management

Looking back, I believe the project's scope should have been smaller. A more focused set of mechanics would have been more appropriate for a project primarily intended for learning.

Choosing the right tools

Working with Unreal Engine also shaped my perspective on its strengths. Although it supports 2D projects, I came away seeing it as better suited to 3D development.
