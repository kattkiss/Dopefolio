Secret Simulator 2004 (2024) (PC)

A puzzle game about finding a hidden game and the login credentials on the family computer.

Engine/Tools: Unity, C#
My Role(s): Programmer/Game Designer
Team Size: 2
Duration: 2 days - Full time
Type: Game Jam project

Download the game here

Description of project

This game was made as part of a game jam hosted by a local student association called Gills.
There were judges consisting of game development teachers from the University of Skövde. The winning categories were:

  • Judges Favorite
  • Best Small Team
  • Best Theme
  • Best of the Jam


The game ended up winning in the Best Theme category.

  • Theme: Secret.
  • Responsibility: Programming, Game Design.

Playtesting & Feedback

The Good

  • An immersive experience
  • Searching through files of a computer was a fun process
  • Players and Judges alike generally replayed it 3 times or more if they didn't win

The Bad

  • Generally difficult to figure out the login credentials
  • Very little feedback to the player when they were on the right track to the solution
  • Wasn't always communicated well to the player what was "just like the real thing" or not, leading to some confusion

Lessons learned

Game specific

  • There should have been a visual notification if the player got one of the fields (Username, Password) right.
  • There should have been something telling the player what the shutdown button does and that they can click n drag windows.

General

  • Trying to simulate an operating system is not as easy as anticipated.
  • Starting off with a very small idea that will work on its own is the way to go for jams. More can always be added later.
